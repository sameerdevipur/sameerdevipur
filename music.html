Music

Music crosses languages, cultures, and borders. A song can communicate something deeply personal even when we do not understand every word, and a melody can carry memories long after it ends. Or it just makes you dance. Or you like it because your brain likes symmetry and rhythm, what do you want from me?

This section is for the albums, artists, songs, and musical moments that have stayed with me, not as definitive reviews, but as personal reflections on what they express, evoke, and make me think about.

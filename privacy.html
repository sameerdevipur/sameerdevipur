Privacy & analytics

This personal website uses Google Analytics only when you choose to allow it. Analytics is disabled by default and no Google Analytics script is loaded before consent.

When allowed, Google Analytics records information such as visited pages, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, and interaction timing. I use aggregated reports to understand which pages are useful and how visitors find the site.
